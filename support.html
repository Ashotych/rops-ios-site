ROPS for iOS — Support

ROPS for iOS is the mobile client for ROPS, the position-based work-object routing system.

Getting access

Accounts are issued by the organisation that operates your ROPS server. There is no public sign-up. If you don't have an account, contact your organisation's ROPS administrator.

Signing in

1. Enter your organisation's server address (for example https://your-server.example), your username and your password.

2. The app remembers the server address for next time.

If sign-in fails, check the server address with your administrator and make sure you are on a network that can reach it.

Common questions

  • I can't see a work object someone shared. Visibility follows your position's permissions on the server. Ask the sender to transfer or circulate it to you.
  • Push notifications? Not yet. Notifications appear inside the app under the menu → Notifications.
  • Arabic? Coming in a later version.
  • Larger text. The app follows the Text Size setting in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size.
  • Leaving or deleting a conversation. Swipe a conversation (or long-press it) and choose Delete. If you started the conversation you own it: you can delete it for every member, or hand it to another member and leave. Other members can leave; the conversation stays for everyone else. The conversation's info screen (tap its name) shows who owns it and lets the owner transfer ownership without leaving.
  • Threads in chat. Long-press a message and choose Reply in thread to answer under it without filling the conversation. The message then shows how many replies it has; tap that to open the thread. Tick “Also send to the conversation” to show one reply in the main conversation too. You follow a thread once you reply in it or someone replies under your message, and the Threads filter in the chat list shows the threads you follow with their unread replies. The bell in a thread turns following on or off. Someone who wrote in a thread can move it to a conversation of its own from the thread's menu. Threads appear when your organisation's server supports them.
  • Quick ways in. Long-press the ROPS icon for New work object, Search, Chat and the number of unclaimed objects on your desk. Add the “My Desk” widget to your home or lock screen to see that number without opening the app (it updates whenever the app runs); its New and camera buttons start a work object. The “New work object” and “New with photo” widgets do the same with one tap: the second opens the camera first, and the photo is attached to the new work object. Work objects also appear in iPhone Spotlight search by reference or title.
  • Acting on several work objects at once. In the Workspace list, open the filter menu and choose Select…, or long-press a row and choose Select…. Tick the objects, then use the bar at the bottom. It only offers the actions that every ticked object allows: if one of them cannot be sealed, Seal is not offered for the selection. Transfer and Circulate ask for the destination once and apply it to all.
  • Attaching files when creating a work object. The New Work Object form has Take photo, Photo library and Files buttons. Files are uploaded right after the work object is created and appear in its history as an attachment.
  • Something went wrong and you want to report it. Open the menu → Settings → Diagnostics (or tap “Diagnostics” at the bottom of the sign-in screen), then Share and send the file to your ROPS administrator. The file lists what the app did and which server requests failed; it contains no passwords and no message text.
