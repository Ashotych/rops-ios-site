ROPS for iOS — Support

ROPS for iOS is the mobile client for ROPS, the position-based work-object routing system.

Getting access

Accounts are issued by the organisation that operates your ROPS server. There is no public sign-up. If you don't have an account, contact your organisation's ROPS administrator.

To explore the app without an account, tap Continue with sample data on the first screen.

Signing in

1. Enter your organisation's server address (for example https://your-server.example), your username and your password.

2. The app remembers the server address for next time.

If sign-in fails, check the server address with your administrator and make sure you are on a network that can reach it.

Common questions

  • I can't see a work object someone shared. Visibility follows your position's permissions on the server. Ask the sender to transfer or circulate it to you.
  • Push notifications? Not yet. Notifications appear inside the app under the menu → Notifications.
  • Arabic? Coming in a later version.
  • Larger text. The app follows the Text Size setting in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size.
