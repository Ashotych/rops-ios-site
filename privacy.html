ROPS for iOS — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Operator: Amara Technology Consultancy LLC ("we", "us")

Contact: art@devs.ae

ROPS for iOS ("the app") is the mobile client for ROPS, a work-object routing system that organisations deploy on their own servers. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

1. Who controls your data

The app connects to a ROPS server operated by your organisation. Your organisation is the data controller for everything you create or see in the app: work objects, comments, attachments, emails and chat messages. We, as the app's publisher, do not receive that data. If you have questions about how your organisation handles it, contact your ROPS administrator.

2. Data the app processes

When you sign in with an account issued by your organisation, the app processes:

  • Account information: your username, display name, user ID, and the positions (roles) assigned to you.
  • Work content: work objects, comments, attachments, emails and links you view or create.
  • Chat: messages, files and photos you send or receive in ROPS chat, and presence/typing status while you use chat.
  • Photos and files: only those you explicitly choose to attach. The app does not access your photo library or files otherwise.
  • Notifications: in-app notifications about work objects you follow.

All of this is stored on your organisation's ROPS server and transmitted only between your device and that server, over HTTPS (TLS).

3. Data stored on your device

The app keeps a session cookie so you stay signed in, your chosen server address and language, and temporary copies of attachments you open. Signing out removes the session. Deleting the app removes all local data.

4. Sample data mode

"Continue with sample data" runs the app with built-in fictional content and no account. In this mode nothing is sent to any server.

5. What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or health data.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No data is sent to the publisher of the app.

6. Retention and deletion

Retention of work content and chat is governed by your organisation's records policy. To delete your account or your data, contact your organisation's ROPS administrator; the app itself has no public sign-up and accounts cannot be self-deleted because they are organisational records.

7. Children

The app is intended for employees of organisations and is not directed at children under 16.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above shows the current version.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: art@devs.ae.
